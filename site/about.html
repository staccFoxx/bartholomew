Our Mission & Engineering Rationale

As frontier language models transition from conversational assistants into autonomous agentic runtimes capable of modifying source trees, executing shell commands, and dispatching live network payloads, traditional perimeter firewalls become ineffective.

Bartholomew develops the foundational software architecture necessary to execute autonomous agents safely. We operate directly within the execution trajectory, parsing and evaluating Abstract Syntax Trees (ASTs) in microsecond timeframes before system calls reach the OS or external environments.
