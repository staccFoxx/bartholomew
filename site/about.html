About Bartholomew Technologies

Engineering Provable Security & Optimization for the Autonomous AI Era

Founded in 2026, Bartholomew Technologies is an independent software lab and research organization dedicated to making autonomous artificial intelligence safe, verifiable, and economically accountable.

As language models evolve from passive chat assistants into autonomous agentic swarms executing code, reading files, and invoking shell commands, traditional network firewalls are no longer sufficient. Bartholomew builds in-process runtime guardrails, deterministic Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) validators, cryptographic Ed25519 capability passkeys, and high-signal prompt engineering tools that empower software developers and enterprises to harness AI without fear of runaway spending, prompt injection, or catastrophic data loss.

Our Core Pillars

Bartholomew Trust Protocol (BTP)

An open standard providing sub-microsecond in-process AST validation, ring-0 kernel guardrails, and deterministic verification for autonomous AI agent tool calls in Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code, and Claude Code.

PromptSmith AI

A friendly, high-precision prompt optimizer and draggable screen assistant. Uses universal domain intelligence across 16 core disciplines to eliminate conversational fluff, enforce role constraints, and benchmark against Claude 3.5, OpenAI o1/o3, and Gemini.

Keystone Capability Passkeys

Cryptographically signed clearance tokens that grant autonomous AI agents fine-grained, auditable permissions across files, commands, and domains with strict real-time budget governors.

Enterprise Compliance & Research

Continuous SOC 2 Type II evidence generation, OWASP LLM-Top-10 mitigation packs, and empirical benchmark studies measuring token efficiency and reasoning fidelity across frontier models.
